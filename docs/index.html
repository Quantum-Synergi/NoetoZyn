This number is deterministically derived by hashing the current Solana slot — it is not a real heartbeat or brainwave. It exists to demonstrate what a real sensor reading would flow through before being masked and hashed above. No sensor, wearable, or biological signal is involved anywhere on this page.

Why this exists

Wearables and health apps stream intimate biological signals into systems you can't inspect or audit. You're asked to trust that the company holding your heart rate, EEG, or activity data won't misuse it — with no independent way to check, and no way to revoke access once it's given.

NoetoZyn is a working proof of concept for a different model: the raw reading never leaves the device in a recoverable form, and what does get published is checkable by anyone, forever, on a public ledger.

How it actually works

1

A reading is taken

A sensor captures something like heart rate or EEG activity. In this demo, that reading is fabricated from the current Solana slot number rather than measured, so the flow can be shown without real hardware.

2

The reading is masked and hashed

Random jitter is added, then the result is passed through a one-way cryptographic hash (SHA-256). The original number can't be recovered from the hash.

3

Only the hash is committed on-chain

The hash, a guardian's wallet signature, and a timestamp are written to a Solana account. Anyone can verify the commitment exists and hasn't been altered — without ever learning the reading it came from.

4

The guardian holds an on-chain switch

A boolean flag on that same account, is_active_shield, can be flipped by the guardian's wallet at any time via a signed transaction — itself a public, auditable action, not a hidden setting.

Where this could go

These are potential directions this kind of infrastructure could support — not features this demo currently implements.

Health insurance

Confirm a policyholder met an activity or vitals threshold, without the insurer ever holding the raw health log that created the liability in the first place.

Workplace wellness programs

Employers could verify program participation to unlock a benefit, without becoming custodians of employee health data they never wanted to store.

Clinical trials and telehealth

Sponsors could get tamper-evident proof that monitoring happened on schedule, without the exposure of holding raw patient signal data.
